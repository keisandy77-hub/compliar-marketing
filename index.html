Why Compliar exists

Built by someone who was doing the job, not selling to it.

Most fire & security software is built for procurement teams at large enterprise contractors. Compliar started differently — as a working tool inside a small fire & security company, shaped around how a 4–6 person team actually gets through a week: a technician checking in on site, filling in a real certificate, and a quote or invoice going out the same day.

No module you'll never switch on. No licence tiers built around headcount you don't have. Just the parts of running the company that take the longest done properly — scheduling, certification, quoting, invoicing, stock, and the paperwork either side of it, all in one system instead of four.

What's inside

Scheduling

See every routine visit before it's due

Day, week and month views of every routine service, tied to the property it's scheduled against — so a maintenance visit never quietly slips past its service interval.

  • Routine visits scheduled per property, not per technician's memory
  • Combined calendar for both office planning and field work

Quotes & invoices

Sales work and defects, kept separate

New business and remedial work found on a routine visit run through two distinct quote types, each with its own numbering sequence — so your win-rate reporting on one doesn't get muddied by the other. Invoices carry your bank details and payment terms automatically.

  • Sales quotes and defect quotes, tracked and numbered separately
  • Print & dispatch straight from the job
  • Company bank details and terms pulled in automatically

Security & isolation

Your data. Only your data.

Every company that signs up gets its own isolated workspace, with tenant isolation enforced at the database — not just hidden behind the interface.

Row-level isolation

Every table is scoped to your organisation at the database layer, so a query simply can't return another company's rows.

Role-based access

Desk and field licences carry different permissions, so office staff and technicians each see exactly what their role needs.

Your own workspace

Sign up and you get a private organisation from the first click — nobody else's certificates, quotes or customers are ever in reach.
